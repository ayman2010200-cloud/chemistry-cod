1- An element from the first transition series, one of its compounds is used in dyes.
- Which of the following choices represents the electronic structure of this element's ion in this compound?
(a) [18Ar] 4s1 3d5
(b) [18Ar] 3d3
(c) [18Ar] 4s2 3d3
(d) [18Ar] 3d2
Answer: (b) [18Ar] 3d3 Explanation: Chromium (Cr) forms Chromium (III) oxide (Cr2O3), which is famously used as a green dye in paints. The neutral Chromium atom has an anomalous configuration [Ar] 4s1 3d5. In the Cr2O3 compound, its oxidation state is +3. Removing 3 electrons (one from 4s and two from 3d) leaves the Cr3+ ion with a configuration of [Ar] 3d3.

(b) Withdrawal 10 mL of water vapor
(c) Doubling the mass of calcium hydroxide

Answer: (b) Withdrawal 10 mL of water vapor Explanation: According to Le Chatelier's Principle, withdrawing a product (water vapor) causes the equilibrium to shift forward (to the right) to compensate for the loss. (b) Element (X) is diamagnetic and its atom contains 6 unpaired electrons.

35- When adding 200 mL of 0.2 M sulphuric acid to 300 mL of 0.2M calcium hydroxide according to the following equation:
H2SO4 + Ca(OH)2 → CaSO4 + 2H2O

- Which of the following expresses the concentration of the remaining substance without reaction?
(a) 0.080 M
(b) 0.040 M
(C) 0.066 M
(d) 0.0100 M
Answer: (b) 0.040 M Explanation: Moles of H2SO4 = 0.2 L × 0.2 M = 0.04 mol.
Moles of Ca(OH)2 = 0.3 L × 0.2 M = 0.06 mol.
The reaction is 1:1, so 0.04 mol of each react. The remaining excess is Ca(OH)2 with 0.02 mol unreacted (0.06 - 0.04).
The total volume of the mixture is 200 + 300 = 500 mL = 0.5 L.
New concentration = moles / volume = 0.02 / 0.5 = 0.04 M.

37- 5L of saturated zinc sulphide (ZnS) solution was cooled from 60°C to 25°C, 1.53 x 10-5 g of the salt is precipitated. If you know that the solubility product Ksp of the salt at 25°C is equal to 1 x 10-21
- Which of the following expresses the solubility product constant (Ksp) of ZnS at 60°C? (ZnS = 97 g/mol)
(a) 1 X 10-15
(b) 1 X 10-21
(C) 3.16X10-8
(d) 3.16X10-11
Answer: (a) 1 X 10-15 Explanation: At 25°C, Ksp = 10-21. Solubility (S) = √Ksp = √10-21 = 3.16 × 10-11 mol/L.
Mass precipitated = 1.53 × 10-5 g. Moles precipitated = (1.53 × 10-5) / 97 = 1.577 × 10-7 mol.
Concentration that precipitated out = 1.577 × 10-7 mol / 5 L ≈ 3.15 × 10-8 mol/L.
Solubility at 60°C = Solubility at 25°C + Concentration precipitated ≈ (3.16 × 10-11) + (3.15 × 10-8) ≈ 3.15 × 10-8 mol/L (since the 25°C solubility is negligible in comparison).
Ksp at 60°C = S2 = (3.15 × 10-8)2 ≈ 9.9 × 10-16, which is closest to 1 × 10-15.

(b) 15.2 minutes
(c) 15.6 minutes
